# 伽

This story came from a town, a courtyard, one something as if it was from an ancient time. There are as like always some golden lights as stars shining from a distant distance. He was a boy, but with a shadow following him, just like a previous phantom passing through himself. It was him; it is me, which following all years named by “Galle”. You are light, you are sunshine, you are everything for me, I have got everything I wanted, but except yourself, who are alone, who was buried by past gravestone, whom soul did past before my appointments.
