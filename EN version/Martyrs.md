#  Martyrs

I long to find works that can move and inspire, and that are compelling enough to keep you reading. But I can't find any, never. Some works have touched me, but the feeling quickly fades. I've only ever experienced this in music. Novel, games, and films—I haven't found anything like it. Why? I want to write such works myself, but when I write, my mind is unsettled, and I'm never satisfied. Why?
I feel that the characters are dead; they haven't truly come to life in my writing.
Philosophy is thinking, but literature is experience; there is a gap in experience.

A fanatical thinker, for the sake of ideals
