#  landscape

The divine realm is vast, so vast that even a mortal could not possibly traverse it in a lifetime. Here, there are towering mountains and flowing rivers, new species, trees that breathe and glow at night, enormous fish that soar through the sky, messengers with triangular eyes in the front and white bodies with wings that can fit letters in the middle, capable of traveling through the void, and everything that once existed in the old world.

But Lin Jing felt as if everything was designed, and that every creature here had the same core.
