#  history

This world certainly has a history, beginning with Ragnarok, and then...

However, the city-states dared not respond, because they did not know whether the other party had good intentions. Once their coordinates were discovered, it would be equivalent to exposing them to the evil spirits' sight.

A powerful being once soared through the heavens, but returned insane, proclaiming the world dead, and soon vanished. Historical records state that he successfully ascended, but his soul remained in the celestial realm.

Looking down upon this world after breaking through the heavens, you'll find that different city-states stand coldly on the wilderness like graveyards. These city-states, of course, float on the wilderness like spheres, emitting a suspicious glow. Between the different continents lie deep seas, seemingly insurmountable chasms, and terrifying monsters lurk in the fissures of these deep seas.

Ultimately, no one knows what lies beyond the black fog; it may be beyond the comprehension of the human species.
