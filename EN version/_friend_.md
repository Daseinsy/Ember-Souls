#  "friend"

Lin Jing has some friends who share his hatred of this environment, but he feels they are not on the same page and doesn't intend to confide in them. He feels these people are only concerned with revealing their true feelings, protecting themselves, and venting their emotions, without considering solutions or doing anything to help others. He feels an invisible barrier between himself and these friends, preventing him from truly connecting with them, but he can't quite put his finger on what this feeling is.

Lin Jing didn't have many friends. While her fellow students ate together, traveled to other parts of the Divine Kingdom, and chatted about which female students were beautiful, whose families were being gossiped about, and which mentors were worth getting to know, Lin Jing always stayed quietly by herself. Lin Jing did have a few close friends, but these friendships were superficial; they generally didn't interact unless there was something to discuss.
