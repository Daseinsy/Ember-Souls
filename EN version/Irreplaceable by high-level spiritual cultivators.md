#  Irreplaceable by high-level spiritual cultivators

Although high-level spiritual cultivators can outthink ordinary people, there is the Sibylis Wall here.

While high-level spiritual practitioners are incredibly fast, both physically and mentally, their thought processes have virtually no upper limit, yet their physical bodies are still subject to time constraints. This means that while spiritual practitioners can perform physical tasks, their speed is limited, and their costs are very high, making ordinary people cheaper than spiritual practitioners.

Therefore, most manual labor and practical scenarios still need to be done by ordinary people, but intellectual activities are increasingly considered to be the privilege of a few, because when the benefits of intellectual activities are not great, people give up their interest in learning, especially when the college assessment has ended.
