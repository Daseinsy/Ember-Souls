# Sadness

A deep sorrow filled his heart; he felt utterly out of place in this world, a loneliness coursing through his veins. He tried to summon a spirit beast, but only managed to conjure a jet-black scythe wreathed in black flames. It was said that not everyone had the chance to summon a spirit beast; those who could were said to possess extraordinary talent, standing above all others, while most ordinary people could only summon common artifacts from the spirit world. Yet, he discovered that he himself was a beast.

This is an utterly desperate world. False temples control people's faith, evil spirits from the universe invade everywhere, cities languish in flames after nightfall, and top powers or families control everything. Some are born as if they possess everything, while others are born the children of slaves. Fallen, crazed evil spirit masters attempt to overthrow everything. There's a small chance of falling into unknown forbidden lands while venturing out to communicate. Red alerts for invasion randomly appear in cities. Each settlement has its own unique characteristics. The ruins of once-glorious civilizations stand in the wilderness. Some yearn to change their fate, yet succumb to madness in the face of powerlessness and endless knowledge. Others live mediocre lives, still unsure of their purpose. Large arrays are set up in the slums, drawing spiritual energy but distributing it to whom? The eyes of the exhausted people are numb. Rebellious witches should be burned alive in the sacred fire—such things happen frequently. No, not so blatantly. Some settlements are carefully controlled, but outwardly orderly. For example, some use knowledge to enhance spiritual power and allow for leaps in spiritual energy—such examples do exist. But what good does it all do? A minority becomes the beneficiaries, then becomes numb, allowing their cronies to rise to power and then turning around to continue oppressing the rest. The crises, whether external or internal, remain unresolved, and humanity makes no progress in this endless cycle of internal strife. Civilization is rapidly being rebuilt thanks to the wealth gained from the wilderness, but what is the point? It's just going around in circles.
However, the dopamine rush is a state of calm, semi-invincible. Or, to put it another way, a dual personality, a protagonist with a split personality. His everyday consciousness is cautious and fearful, while his divine consciousness is invincible, alluring, calm, and cold. This divine consciousness always intervenes when the protagonist is in danger, but the protagonist is never aware of this, only believing he has a protagonist's halo and is simply lucky enough to escape danger every time. Everyone has a profession or title above their head, but the protagonist's title is: The Chosen One.


Yes, the microscopic and macroscopic nature of things dictates that even a grain of sand is unimaginably complex, and every person is a unique and complex individual. But what we are talking about is the soul. The souls of most things are impoverished, ridiculous, and utterly mediocre, no matter what position they reach. Interesting souls are pitifully rare.
One day, I suddenly realized I didn't want to be one of those mediocre, laughable people, trapped in the boring routines of self and society, endlessly chasing after education, marriage, social obligations, and procreation. Where is there any human nobility or sanctity in that? To be bound by such things for a lifetime, even one's soul would be saddled and covered in dust. Most people would be grateful if they could simply extend their love for their blood, their own self, or their country to the love for all living beings. However, love is difficult. Many are trapped in their own little circles, incredibly stingy with such kindness, because they consider it saintly. They only extend kindness to those they deem worthy of help. Such people might be called good people, but they are not the ones we are looking for. Yet, only the saintly are truly sacred among humanity.

If consciousness is copied, am I still me? Of course I am, because everyone's spiritual energy is unique, a one-of-a-kind, unchangeable code bestowed by the heavens. Even if you copy the same way of thinking, you are still someone else.

However, when one being surpasses another in power or even in the dimension of life, that fragile empathy and balance easily become a joke.

Ragnarok

A ranch, cattle and sheep oblivious to their fate and meaning, a seemingly omnipotent hand of divine punishment, a town shrouded in mist, humans returning from their wanderings acting strangely, spirits being constantly drained, generation after generation of humanity, predetermined destinies, pseudo-humans, lovable people, sunlight streaming through the clouds, hysteria, amnesia, the so-called saint. The world possessed by the lord, the world in which the lord lives, the lord of the world in which the lord lives, deathly silence, despair, a cognitive miasma, the awakened, the heretics, a game without an ending, nothing new, a history dominated by desire and power, repetitive slaughter, a game of fate. The only beloved daughter of an ultimate lord, she chose to lose her memories and descend to the mortal realm out of compassion. She possesses infinite life, but besides that, she has nothing. The lord will not help her, because he believes the harm inflicted on his daughter by NPCs is illusory. He has no gender, because he is a god. He lacks normal human emotions, because he is a god, because he can directly erase cause and effect. He only hopes that his only daughter can experience reincarnation, attain the Great Dao, and ultimately ascend to godhood. He is not a dictator, nor does he have desires. He is one of the ultimate truths, the embodiment of the Heavenly Dao. He only coldly observes everything. She will only reincarnate again and again. Each time she dies, the lord will erase everyone's memories of her and send her to the next reincarnation, until she will relinquishes emotions. She doesn't know who she is, what her name is, or where she comes from. There is a fire in her heart, but she doesn't know what that fire is. However, in the process of living, she gradually learns what that indescribable thing is. She has long, fiery red hair and eyes as deep as the night.
Every living being possesses its own unique spirit. Legend says that when a creature dies, its spirit ascends to the heavens, enters the underworld, and merges into the River Styx. The River Styx actually has two parts: the first is the River Styx itself, and the second is the River of the Living, also known as the River of Time. These two rivers flow parallel to each other but never meet; their origins and destinations are unknown. Legend also says that every star in the sky is a spirit of the dead. People on earth can connect with these stars through sincere prayer, causing the corresponding star to vanish. The worlds possessed by different lords seem separated by an endless mist. Lords can descend to the mortal realm, but people cannot see them, just as ants drawn on paper can never look up at the sky. People interpret the natural disasters caused by these lords as divine wrath and offer their spirits in prayer.
The Sovereigns can erase time, space, cause and effect, and any trace of a spirit's existence. Even the river of time is a toy to them. They are transcendent of time. In their eyes, there is no time, only a series of records, just like a program. A program can run but can also be recorded and backed up. It can be stopped, deleted, restored, accelerated, skipped, or modified at any time. Of course, the Sovereigns also have their own time, but their time is completely different from the time of the Sons of Man.
She will lose, yet with each of her reincarnations, she will retain all her memories and spirit.
She seemed to see an entire starry sky, each light a world unto itself. She couldn't tell how many stars there were, because she was surrounded by a vast sea of stars. Growing larger and larger, and then an ever-growing world crashing towards her.
This is an epic tale, an attempt to depict the world. You might tire of endless cycles, yet you can always find a new and exciting world. But the vastness of time will inevitably lead to endless boredom and weariness, until she resorts to suicide to reincarnate. Each reincarnation brings her to a new world, bestowing upon her a new identity, but she can never find her way back. All regrets are irreparable; everything is in the past. A lord can stop or speed up time, but cannot reverse it. Reversing time requires going against the current, which demands resisting the impact of the entire river of time. Even if the spiritual energy required is recorded in billions, it can only rewind a lord's world by a few seconds. Time reversal is the power of only the gods. However, there is no absolute time; the time in each world is different. They merely begin at a starting point and then flow on.
There is something called a time portal that can connect different worlds, and creatures from those worlds can travel through this portal to other worlds.
However, her spirit is as indestructible as a star's core, and cannot even be shaken. Therefore, even under the erosion of time, it never wears down but only becomes more and more beautiful. Her appearance never ages, but only changes, becoming more mature and refined. Only her own heart can make her grow old, yet her heart seems to have never aged.
She can choose to reincarnate into the current world indefinitely, but she cannot stay in one world indefinitely, because the lifespan of a world is also finite. Lords will age, worlds will wither and decay, and every world will eventually be destroyed. When the spirit dries up, the world will collapse and scatter into countless points of light returning to the starry sky.
The miasma of cognition is such that even if the ruler freezes time, the beings within it will not perceive that time ever stopped. A hundred years of solitude, a thousand years of loneliness, a moment of utter ruin. Humans raise livestock, but who is raising humans?
Looking at the desolation and complexity before him, the crying, laughing, and angry faces, those different familiar faces seemed to intertwine into an inseparable image, like a huge, bizarre eye reflecting all the scenes of this world. He remembered a song from his past life.
