#  Flower of Evil

%%
Lin Jing knew Luo Mingyuan's mindset. He knew it from the first glance, because in a sense, he was Luo Mingyuan from decades ago. Lin Jing just wondered, if this were the case, would he also become a dragon in the future? The academy was filled with such intoxicating power, and the people there, in order to curry favor with the powerful figures above and that hidden deity, did many things against their conscience. Many things in their hands were not for the purpose of fairness and justice, but for the purpose of maximizing their own interests.
Something happened at the college recently that really touched Lin Jing.
Although the academy believes that students should not kill each other, if the person in question is of significant importance in the story, or if the entire group is involved in setting a trap, the guilty can be exempted from punishment by collectively paying an IOU to the divine authority—euphemistically called an indulgence. This is what the divine kingdom calls an equivalent exchange.
