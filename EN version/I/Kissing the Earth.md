#  Kissing the Earth

The earth, according to ancient ballads, is the body of a sleeping behemoth. Earthquakes are its murmurs, volcanoes its pulse, and the deep fissures are the folds it creases as it turns before awakening. Sometimes, the cold voice of the heavens echoes in one's ears, as if a thousand whispers simultaneously: "I grant you an immortal soul of gold, to experience joy and sorrow in the cycle of reincarnation." Then comes silence, in which forgotten names flicker in the depths of memory, like gold coins in a sunken ship at the bottom of the sea.

Even novice cultivators at the initial stage of spiritual cultivation—those young people who have just learned to guide the flow of spiritual energy through their meridians—yearn to become masters. Their dreams are small and humble, like fireflies in the bushes, but they are a priceless passion, untouched by hardship. They pick up stone shards as swords, slashing at withered trees, shouting out the names of moves they've heard from legends. Passing old cultivators scoff, but they don't care. Someday, someday.

"Don't cry, Heath." The girl in the white dress whispered, sitting by the porthole of the transmigrating spaceship, holding a fading Mana coin in her hands. Outside, the starry sea flowed, seven worlds passing by the porthole in succession—cyber neon, the wheat fields of the manor, the cold light of the ice plains—they would connect when the seven stars converged, and then separate again, like fingers closing and opening.

All aspirations that transcend everything will eventually vanish in the wind.

And those nameless guardians, those girls who turned into light, those faces forgotten and remembered in reincarnation, will continue to fight against the only and eternal disease of poverty on this land without bacteria, viruses, or cellular rebellion, until Mana dries up, until the starry sea collapses, until one day, a novice in the initial stage of enlightenment finally splits open the withered tree that blocks his view and discovers a key hidden deep in its heart.

The shape of the key is exactly the same as the rhythmic light in the abyss of the rift.

He didn't know what it was.

But he will find out.
