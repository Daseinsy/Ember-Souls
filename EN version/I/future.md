#  future

If the future of the city-state truly no longer requires manual labor, and most affairs can be handled by the spiritual entities created by spiritual cultivators, then where will the majority of those at the bottom of society find their way out? Without work, how will they survive? Perhaps they can only retreat to the most primitive form of self-sufficiency, barely maintaining the most basic sustenance; or perhaps they can only rely on the minimum relief funds provided by the city-state to eke out a living; or perhaps they can huddle together within their families for mutual support and warmth; and there is an even narrower path, which is to sell their own spiritual power to obtain meager capital, thereby exploring that sliver of possibility of breaking through the shackles of social class.
