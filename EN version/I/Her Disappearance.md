#  Her Disappearance

If he could stay by her side and protect her forever, could he have changed the outcome? The answer is no; Norwegian Wood had already hinted at the answer.

Born into an ordinary family, she was hurt as a child and her family suffered a sudden change. As a result, she doesn't pursue love, is sensitive and suspicious, and even feels unworthy. She tries her best to become stronger, which has become an obsession for her. However, she is calm to the point of madness. To outsiders, she appears peaceful, beautiful, and glamorous, but in reality, her heart is burning and riddled with holes.

One step she took would lead to the abyss. She didn't want to hurt others, so she chose to hurt herself to kill those evil things.

Her soul was so noble that the world was unworthy of her existence. She sought revenge, but after achieving it, she suddenly lost her bearings. The support that had sustained her crumbled, and she began to self-destruct frequently. Then, she met Guang, Lin Jing, but Lin Jing couldn't stay with her forever. Until, until she saw millions of people suffering in the world, she chose to accept a sacrifice—not the kind of sacrifice, but one that sowed seeds of hope across the earth. Her soul ascended to the heavens and she eventually became a sage.

The deity failed to advance and became an evil god, seeking to destroy everything. Just as the divine kingdom was about to become a forbidden land, she stepped forward, her voice gathering and the collective will of all living beings to build an iron wall that pierced the evil god's unseen heart, bringing the sun to shine through the clouds. However, the divine kingdom was no longer ruled by a god. But it is said that those who sacrificed themselves became heroic spirits, guarding this land.
Therefore, whenever it snows, the entire city is covered in silver overnight, and the whole city will stop working to hold a grand ice and snow festival.
