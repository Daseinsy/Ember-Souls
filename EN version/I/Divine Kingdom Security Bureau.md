#  Divine Kingdom Security Bureau

Surprisingly, while the Divine Kingdom's Security Bureau claims to maintain social order and security, and in practice largely exists to achieve this goal, their attitude towards illegal and substandard spirit stones is quite subtle. Substandard spirit stones refer to cheap, large-quantity spirit energy obtained from corpses, cattle, or sheep.
There's a strange paradox in the psychic world: things you can easily lift with your hands might require a high level of spiritual power to move. Conversely, things you can't do with your hands are easily done with spiritual power.
