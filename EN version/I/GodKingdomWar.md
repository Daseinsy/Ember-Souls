# GodKingdomWar

It is said that the surrounding city-states committed some great crime, and the people there eagerly hoped that the Kingdom of God would take over the rule of the old gods. Although not everyone said so, there were always a few voices, but no one cared about the truth. The gods of the Kingdom of God always wanted to expand their territory, and the fallen gods were always killed quickly. The blood of the gods was spilled all over the land, while the old people bathed in everything and enjoyed the joy of revelry.

The gods took over this place and established a court to bring the guilty to justice. They also built a new temple, and the fire began to burn. People began to pray, and wherever there was fire, there were divine pronouncements, regardless of the medium through which the message was disseminated.
