#  The Root of Evil Spirits

Evil spirits are not some accidental calamity, but one of the fundamental laws of this world. They are born alongside life, like shadows to light.

There are three main sources of evil spirits. First, they are born in the Mirror Realm—a spiritual dimension parallel to the phenomenal world, where evil spirits grow naturally like plants, and seep into the human world from certain cracks. Second, they are born from the souls of the dead in the phenomenal world—souls burdened with deep-seated obsessions and lingering resentment, which, if not properly guided, will corrupt into evil spirits. Third, and most universally, they are born from the evil thoughts within a group. Human goodness and evil always coexist; this is a fundamental law of the phenomenal world. Evil spirits feed on the evil thoughts and suffering of living beings, and these things allow them to grow stronger. Therefore, the existence of evil spirits, like day and night, is an immutable natural law.

Evil spirits possess a meager level of intelligence, though not high, enough to lure those driven to desperation. They are adept at observation, at choosing their prey, and at extending their tentacles when their prey is most vulnerable. High-level evil spirits can even impersonate humans, fabricating an entire city—the pedestrians on the street, the tavern owner, the church priest—all are phantoms created by the evil spirit. Only one living person is trapped within, completely unaware, like in The Truman Show. The flaw in this disguise lies in time—evil spirits cannot maintain the illusion for longer than a certain period, and they do not understand the subtleties of human emotions, occasionally displaying stiff expressions and giving irrelevant answers.

The hierarchy of evil spirits is not clearly defined, but there are distinct levels of strength. At the bottom are ordinary evil spirits, acting on instinct like wild beasts, chasing after the scent of pain and fear. Mid-level evil spirits possess a degree of intelligence, capable of devising simple traps and schemes. High-level evil spirits—including those at the Annihilation level—inhabit areas known as Forbidden Zones of Life. Most of these Forbidden Zones were born on the Day of Decay, and behind each one lies the sacrifice of countless lives.

Why hasn't the forbidden zone of life continued to expand? Or why has its expansion been so slow? Because of the existence of heroic spirits. Those who sacrificed themselves did not truly depart—they became the souls of the land through sacrifice, and their immense willpower slumbered most of the evil spirits. The heroic spirits relinquished reincarnation and peace, guarding this land in the form of spirits. Their existence is silent, like roots underground, unseen yet supporting a city. Countless armies are stationed along the borders, and each nation has built psionic walls along its coastal borders , led by powerful beings at the Sovereign Realm. The seas are teeming with demons; this is truly a forbidden zone of life. Communication and transportation between nations rely entirely on expensive psionic legendary arrays. Large armies are regularly dispatched daily to clear the oceans to prevent the demonic forces from growing too strong. Yet, even so, there are records of land being breached by demonic armies.

There is great terror in the darkness. Close your eyes when it gets dark—a great terror lurks in that deep darkness. When the seven tectonic plates exist in completely different dimensions, the barriers of the phenomenal world reach their weakest point, and even daytime is no longer safe. Walking in the twilight light will drive a person mad; it is a near-eternal loneliness and desolation. Therefore, settlements must have torches, sacred flames to dispel evil—but that light is too weak, only illuminating three feet beneath one's feet. Beyond that, there is darkness.

To avoid public unrest, out of fear of personal retribution, or out of adherence to historical tradition, federal officials unanimously concealed the truth about the evil spirits. Civilians were simply told "don't go out at night," but not why. The more people knew, the more silent they became, because the truth weighed heavier than fear—the evil spirits could not be eradicated; wherever there was life, there would be conflict and malice, and where there was malice, new evil spirits would spring up.
