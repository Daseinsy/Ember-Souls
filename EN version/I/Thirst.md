#  Thirst

There is a kind of aging that is not the aging of someone nearing death, but a kind of exhaustion. It manifests as a young person with a strong spirituality, yet feeling extremely thirsty—a thirst on a spiritual level. It's like a fiercely burning celestial fire, in its prime, easily exhausted due to a lack of fuel. The blazing sun in one's heart doesn't die of old age, but of thirst. A person whose spiritual essence is too pure has extremely high demands for spiritual energy, to the point that ordinary methods cannot satisfy them.

Lin Jing was like that. He was simultaneously urged by a strong impulse to cultivate, as if he would die if he stopped. In fact, his spirit was really dying. The billowing smoke from the incomplete burning of the great fire in his heart could only be seen by himself.

Therefore, he often had an impulse to destroy everything. It wasn't about destroying everything, but about filling the extreme thirst in his heart through destruction and devouring. Because destruction has such a magical power, nature seems to reward destruction from the outset, and the pleasure it brings can even temporarily eliminate spiritual alarms, just like an evil spirit.

An evil spirit said to Lin Jing, "Join us."

However, Lin Jing forcefully suppressed that impulse, but fire doesn't go out just because it's suppressed.
