#  Elites are everywhere

In the college, even if you just throw a brick at someone, the person you hit might be someone with a very impressive background.
But how to describe these makeshift groups? In terms of spiritual power, these people may all be giants, wielding terrifying strength, but most of them are very superficial and have risen to their positions through the power of faith. Their spirituality may actually be quite ordinary.
Therefore, many people only look at the results, even avoiding spirituality, and have developed a kind of faith intelligence to be on par with spiritual talent. Those who rely solely on spirituality may be very powerful in themselves, but without the support of status, they are generally the ones who are ostracized.
You'll find that these so-called elites are all repulsive creatures of relationships, and they form a vast network of mutual interests.
