#  Pecking at dust

Why is it that some people are born with, or at least have, a healthy body and exceptional social talent, enjoying almost a smooth and lucky life, while others struggle their entire lives only to be relegated to the dust, even after burning themselves out, being labeled as lazy and unambitious failures? Why are talented people often buried, while only the victors and the lucky ones are ever seen? It's not that there's only one moyan every few decades, but that countless others like them never have the chance to showcase their talents before dying alone in the endless night. Why does this world seem like a bizarre makeshift stage, where those lovable people are never truly appreciated by the masses? Countless heroes have sacrificed their lives, yet the public only remembers the names of kings, generals, and grand organizations.
It's not that those lovely people didn't try to make the world a better place, but rather that their voices were frozen in the unanimous indifference of the masses.
