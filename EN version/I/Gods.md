#  Gods

The reason why gods were eventually born is not surprising. It was the fear of various natural disasters and the impulse to interpret the world that humans in ancient times had. Powerful individuals born from faith eventually ascended the ladder to godhood and became gods.
This was especially true after the apocalypse. In this dying world, people preferred to worship a false god rather than be freely exiled to the wilderness. The history of Ragnarok was deliberately downplayed and its specific details erased, leaving only the story of how the new gods tried to turn the tide.
The gods have almost absolute power; if they say you are guilty, then you are guilty.
