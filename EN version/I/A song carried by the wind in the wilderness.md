#  A song, carried by the wind in the wilderness

She was one of those people who dreamt of this song while she was in a well, and she wrote the lyrics herself. The lyrics roughly conveyed this meaning: Why sing it in the wilderness? Because it's an evil spirit. The wilderness will remember, all things will grow, and then a new kingdom without God will spring up.
Even the sages were awakened by the song, which transcended the barriers of time and space, bringing their wandering souls here. At that moment, the wilderness fell silent, and even the mad savages ceased their cries.
At that moment, flowers bloomed on the barren wasteland, and countless particles drifted down from the sky, gradually tearing through the darkness. Evil spirits emerged from the void, then trembled and transformed into countless points of light. Demons were betrayed by the evil spirits, and those who came upon hearing the news were devoured. A crescent sun rose in the sky.
A well appeared on the ground at some unknown time. Some unseen things emerged from it, and voices gathered together, some ancient and some gentle. They were like large hands clasped together, gradually merging into the ocean of songs.
