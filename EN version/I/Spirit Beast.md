# Spirit Beast

Spirit beasts are magical creatures. Legend has it that they originate from the spirit realm, where all life consciousness has resided, possessing magical powers surpassing those of the human body. Spirit beasts are born through gestation, not pre-existing beings; no one knows exactly how they are born. Legend says that when evil spirits first invaded the world, the mortal human body was almost powerless against them. At that moment, an extremely strong belief brought the world's first spirit beast into reality. Surrounded by flames, formless and intangible, it ended the supernatural era in one fell swoop. Later generations called it "Xin" (薪).

Even now, every settlement has a temple dedicated to "Fire." Sacred flames burn within these temples, capable of dispelling most evil or reducing its occurrence. However, spirit beasts can fall. The process of a spirit beast's fall is called "Sorrow," and its spirit trainer is therefore known as an evil spirit trainer.
