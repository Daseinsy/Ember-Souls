#  Initial state of mind

Some beginners can summon their own spirit beasts and use spiritual power to move objects with mass around them. The extent of the movement depends on the resistance. Generally speaking, the higher the mass or the stronger the spirituality of the object, the more difficult it is to move. It is said that this kind of mental movement requires resisting both the laws of physics and the will of the being moved.
In addition, cultivators at the initial stage of cultivation can reduce their food intake and excretion by absorbing the spiritual energy of heaven and earth.
Furthermore, the brain is nourished, which improves multi-threaded processing speed and reaction speed. However, it will be different in a different body. This is mainly due to hardware, but the real improvement is in thinking ability and spiritual intuition.
It is said that spirituality is the soul of a spiritual cultivator. The soul of a mortal is deeply bound to the body, so when the body dies, the soul also vanishes, only not completely disappeared but transferred to another dimension, one might call the underworld. While the physical body of a spiritual cultivator dies, the soul can still function and think for a short period, but without the five senses, not even basic touch. It's like being trapped in a boundless black box. At this time, the speed of thought might be basic, but extremely slow compared to before, like a cart without wheels that can only be pushed with difficulty. In reality, although there are differences depending on the level of cultivation, high-level spiritual cultivators can still think very quickly. Although one can see the soul through spiritual vision, it's like a ghost detached from the phenomenal world, rejected by the world. If it can't hold on, it will be completely expelled or devoured by evil spirits. Therefore, a suitable physical body must be found as soon as possible. However, the found body must have a certain compatibility; otherwise, even if one possesses another, they cannot integrate. While a temporary emergency body is acceptable, or even a corpse, it must be a recently deceased body still possessing vitality.
