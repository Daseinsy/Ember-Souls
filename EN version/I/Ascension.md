#  Ascension

Legend has it that when a person is near their limit, they can unleash unimaginable energy. Some can even leap several levels to gain immense power, but the price is that their soul, like a dying ember, quickly turns to ashes after a brief flash, or even vanishes completely, leaving no trace in the world. This state is called ascension, but the end of ascension is not immortality, but nothingness and death.
