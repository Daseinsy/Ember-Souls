# Media

The role of media and popular culture is to create topics, stir up emotions, generate conflict, gain traffic, earn revenue, and incidentally instill ideology.
This is the emptiness of a series of processes.

Capital is busy making money, students are busy racking up grades, workers are busy earning wages, and patients are busy saving their lives. How many people really care about carbon neutrality? Carbon emissions are just a derivative issue of some major problems.
