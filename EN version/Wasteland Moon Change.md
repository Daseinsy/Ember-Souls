#  Wasteland Moon Change

Darkness, like solidified ink, pressed down from all directions.

There were no roads on the wasteland, only pebbles and withered grass roots. Each landing was a heavy thud, making Lin Jing's bones ache. She lay on Gamma's back, her face buried in the rough white fur, her throat filled with the taste of rust.

The wind sliced the sounds of the pursuers behind them into fragments, sending them over intermittently.

"Lin Jing—stop running! Your summoned beast can't run anymore, can it?"

"It's just a white dog, how dare it venture into the depths of the wilderness?"

A sharp laugh drifted in the wind. Lin Jing didn't turn around, but she could sense specks of psychic energy in the distance, like a swarm of hungry fireflies, flickering behind her. She squeezed her legs together, her fingers unconsciously tugging at the hair on the back of Gamma's neck. The hair, matted with cold sweat and blood, clung coldly to her palm.

Gamma didn't make a sound. It just ran.

Lin Jing was familiar with this silence. It had been silent since it was a kitten, a round snowball curled up in the center of her spirit array, its eyes barely open, only rubbing its wet nose against her fingers. Later, it grew into a large white dog, waist-high—no, she always thought it was a dog. There were no white dogs in the spirit beast lineage; some said it was a mutated snow mastiff, others said it wasn't even a spirit beast. Gamma never argued; it simply followed at her feet, its bushy tail sweeping the ground. When other spirit beasts pounced on it, it would simply sidestep, pressing its belly against her legs, never fighting back.

So until this moment, Lin Jing had never considered that there might be other possibilities.

A whooshing sound suddenly came from behind. Lin Jing instinctively looked down, and a bluish-white spirit blade grazed the back of her head, severing a few strands of hair. A burnt smell filled the air.

"Run! Keep running! I want to see where a piece of trash who can't even protect a spirit beast can run to!"

The pursuers' voices drew closer. Lin Jing heard their footsteps, mixed with the buzzing of psionic energy, like a swarm of wild bees. Someone flanked them from the side; it was a tall, thin man, holding a ball of swirling wind psionic energy in his hand, a blue light illuminating half his face, his smile twisted.

"Hand over the stuff, Lin Jing. Otherwise, you white dog will become a pile of rotten flesh in the wasteland tonight."

As he spoke, the wind spirit had already left his hand. Gamma spun sharply, throwing Lin Jing to the side, nearly causing her to fall. But the wind blade still grazed Gamma's left hind leg. A very low whimper escaped Gamma's throat, and she staggered, her speed suddenly slowing down.

More pursuers closed in. Azure, red, and white lights flickered in the darkness, like a noose slowly tightening. Lin Jing finally saw them clearly—five people, all dressed in matching grey robes with ferocious beast patterns embroidered on the collars. The leader was a bald man with a sinister gaze; he held no psionic energy, simply standing with his hands behind his back, as if watching a hunt that had already ended.

"Lin Jing," the bald man spoke, his voice low but clear enough to cut through the wind, "you rode a dog across the wasteland for half the night, aren't you tired?"

A low chuckle rose from the surroundings.

Lin Jing, panting, slid off Gamma's back. She lost her balance, her knees buckling, and she half-knelt on the ground. Gamma immediately turned around, shielding her with its body. Blood was flowing from its left hind leg; dark red liquid trickled down its white fur, pooling at its paw. Its ears were pressed back, its tail drooping rather than tucked, and a low growl emanated from its throat.

“Gamma…” Lin Jing reached out and touched its warm body. It was trembling.

The bald man stepped forward, his gaze landing on Gamma as if assessing a piece of merchandise. "This spirit beast... is not bad. It's a pity it followed the wrong master. Hand over that item, and I might spare its life."

“It’s not on me,” Lin Jing said. Her voice was hoarse, like sandpaper rubbing against her throat.

"Stubborn to the end." The bald man sighed and winked at the person beside him. The tall, thin man immediately raised his hand, and wind spiritual energy condensed again, brighter and faster than before, like a cyan blade.

"I'll ask you one more time, where's the stuff?"

Lin Jing didn't answer. She simply hugged Gamma's neck. At that moment, her forehead rested against Gamma's neck, and she could feel the blood surging in its veins, like an underground river. Gamma turned its head and nuzzled her ear with its nose. Its deep brown eyes looked at her in the darkness, quiet and docile, just like countless nights before.

But the next second, the wind blade arrived.

Lin Jing saw light. A bluish-white light, like a thin fragment of the moon, slashed towards them. She closed her eyes and buried her face in Gamma's fur.

Then she heard a cracking sound.

It wasn't that she was hit. Something inside her body shattered.

The sound was indescribable, like a glass wall shattering deep within her soul, from which psychic energy surged forth. Her heart clenched, as if gripped by an invisible hand, then abruptly released. Blood rushed to her limbs, her fingertips tingling. She felt a burning sensation, not her own, surge from her chest to her arms, to Gamma's skin.

Gamma raised her head and let out a low howl.

The howl seemed to come from the depths of an ancient wasteland, shaking the ground slightly. Lin Jing opened her eyes and saw Gamma's body glowing—first a pale silver-white, then rapidly intensifying, like moonlight solidified. Its bones deformed and stretched in the light, and the muscles bulging at its shoulder blades writhed violently. Lin Jing almost thought it was going to die, but the whimper gradually transformed into another sound, deep and majestic, like a sleeping giant turning over.

"What's going on..." The tall, thin man took two steps back. The wind blade had already left his hand, but it was shredded in mid-air by some unseen force, turning into specks of blue light and dissipating.

The bald man's expression changed. For the first time, he showed emotion and growled, "Stop talking nonsense, attack together!"

Five beams of spiritual energy lit up simultaneously, hurtling towards them from all directions.

Gamma suddenly arched its back. Lin Jing was lifted up by a gentle force and landed back on its back. This time, she felt that Gamma's body had grown larger, its muscles as solid as cast iron. Its four paws sank deep into the soil, and it pushed off with a powerful thrust.

Enormous wings unfurled from its shoulder blades with a roar, pure white as snow, each feather shimmering with silver light. The instant the wings unfolded, the resulting wind pressure knocked the nearest enemy to the ground. Gamma soared into the air, the dark wasteland rapidly shrinking beneath its feet.

A full moon appeared in the night sky at some unknown time.

The moon was abnormally large, its edges tinged with an eerie dark red, like a half-closed eye, coldly hanging above the desolate plain. The moonlight wasn't white, but silvery-red, like a thin mist, silently pouring down.

Lin Jing lay on Gamma's back, gazing up at the moon. She had never seen a moon like this before. The moonlight fell on Gamma's wings, making each feather shine like a blade.

Gamma swooped down.

It was too fast; Lin Jing could only cling tightly to its fur, closing and reopening her eyes. She saw Gamma open its mouth, moonlight condensing into a silver-red sphere, which it then spat out with a roar. The sphere crashed onto the wasteland without an explosion, only a silent shockwave that spread out instantly like ripples on water. The moonlight fell on the gray-robed men, like countless tiny blades, cutting through flesh and bone. Their screams were swallowed by the silver-red light before they could even be heard.

The wasteland fell silent instantly.

Only the sound of the wind and Gamma's heavy breathing could be heard.

Lin Jing looked down. The grass and stones on the ground had been overturned, as if plowed by a giant plow, leaving deep furrows. The gray-robed men were gone, leaving only a few charred hems of their clothing, like dead butterflies lying in the cracks of the rocks.

Gamma slowly landed back on the ground. Its wings folded and lay flat against its sides, but it didn't disappear. Its size didn't return to normal; it remained a giant white wolf, taller than a horse. It turned around and gently touched Lin Jing's hand with its nose.

Lin Jing knelt on the ground, staring blankly at it. Moonlight shone from behind it, edging its white fur with a silvery edge. Its eyes were no longer the gentle dark brown, but had turned into a very pale gold, like melted amber, reflecting the eerie full moon.

“Gamma…” she called out in a hoarse voice.

Gamma lowered its head and, as always, nuzzled her cheek with its wet nose. Its breath was warm, carrying the scent of the dry wind and moonlight from the wasteland. Lin Jing opened her mouth, wanting to say something, but found her throat felt like it was choked by something. She finally understood that this white dog she had always thought of was actually a wolf that could spread its wings under the night sky.

The moon still hung overhead, coldly illuminating the silent wasteland. In the distance, a faint gray tinge had already appeared on the horizon. Lin Jing buried her face in Gamma's soft neck hair, her voice so soft it seemed to dissipate in the wind.

"Let's go home."

Gamma responded softly, like a gentle sob, or the howl of a newborn wolf.
