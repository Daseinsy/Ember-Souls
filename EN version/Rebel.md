#  Rebel

The madman devised a meticulous plan to destroy the world. Outwardly, he appeared to be a powerful, high-ranking, and gentle spiritual cultivator. But in reality, he had been secretly plotting a decade-long urge to destroy the world, not for his own sake, but to drag all living beings of this world down with him. He believed that death would end the fighting, the repulsive hypocrites, the man-made suffering, and the cycle of reincarnation orchestrated by the despicable victors. If the world was already hopelessly corrupt, then only destruction could save it.

There is a rebel organization in the Kingdom of God whose doctrine is to destroy the whole world, but some of its leaders want to take over.

The rebellious organization was eventually suppressed, but it tore a wound that could not be ignored within the divine kingdom. That wound already existed, but it became larger and deeper. Moreover, the divine kingdom ultimately sacrificed the people of an entire region in the process of suppressing the rebellion, and no one dared to mention that incident.

Some of the illustrious individuals shouted their support at the event, though they were quickly silenced, their voices have not been forgotten.
