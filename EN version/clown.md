#  clown

Jack always wore a clown mask. He used to be a very kind and compassionate person, until one day he felt utterly hopeless about the world. At that time, he was seduced by the devil and made a deal with him. He no longer believed in truth, goodness, and beauty; he was just a clown in a mask. He slaughtered an entire village, covered buildings with graffiti in blood, and rose to the upper echelons of society, manipulating the fate of the lower classes at will.

There is a city where everyone is a phantom created by a higher evil spirit. Only one living person lives there, exchanging pleasantries with his neighbors, buying bread at the market, and feeding pigeons on a bench in the evening, completely unaware that those smiles and wings are all part of an meticulously woven dream. This is The Truman Show, but it is more cruel than any stage—the evil spirits are simply curious about what it would sound like for a real heart to beat in utter falsehood.

The famous singer took to the stage accompanied by a sacred music choir. Legend has it that these hymns can ward off the corruption of evil spirits—when the contamination of cults spreads to the outskirts of towns, the choir stands on the city walls and sings, the sound waves rippling like water, dissolving the evil spirits like salt in rain. But no one knows where the power of the sacred music comes from; perhaps it comes from faith, perhaps from the resonance of mana, or perhaps evil spirits simply dislike beautiful sounds.

Life and death, illness and disaster, have their own rhythm in this world. Some use AI to write the future—the Prophecy Machine of the Star Federation is as vast as a palace, with billions of gears turning, spitting out slips of paper printed with tomorrow's words, but the writing on the slips gradually fades after leaving the machine. Occasionally, a powerful cultivator awakens within a novice youth, who suddenly understands an ancient sword technique one morning, golden light emanating from his fingertips—but as the sun sets, the memory vanishes like mist; he only remembers having what seemed like a very long dream. The souls of the departed guard the earth, sometimes appearing as transparent silhouettes under the moonlight, sometimes condensing into dewdrops on morning grass, silent, simply watching.
