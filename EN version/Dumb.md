# Dumb

Some children are mute, or they are deaf from birth, so it's not that they can't speak, they just don't know how to speak because they have never heard any sound.

Being confined in the temple was unbearable. It was a black cage where your five senses were still intact, but it was no different from losing them. All you could see was endless darkness, and all you could hear was your own heartbeat. It was so lonely it was maddening.
