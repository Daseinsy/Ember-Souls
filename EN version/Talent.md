# Talent

On one side are the seemingly transcendent, almost inhuman, divine beings; on the other side are the ordinary, toiling masses still struggling to survive. Amidst this division and imbalance, where does human value lie? Perhaps some choose to drift along, utterly acknowledging their own uselessness; perhaps some, in boundless despair, stubbornly nurture new hope; perhaps some, confident in their talent, remain perpetually unappreciated; perhaps some simply indulge in a life of debauchery, escaping reality; or perhaps many more simply retreat into silence and numbness, ultimately left with only one belief—that as long as one lives, that's enough.

The idea of being talented but unrecognized is almost impossible in modern times. Because in this information age, the chance of being seen gradually increases to nearly 100% as long as you survive. You might be unseen for a period, but you won't remain unseen forever. Unless you completely avoid showcasing yourself, but that's not considered being talented but rather a deliberate choice to withdraw from the public eye.
