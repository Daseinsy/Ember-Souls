#  High-level spiritual cultivator

High-level spiritual cultivators possess extremely fast thinking and reaction speeds, roughly equivalent to modern AI. The ordinary phenomenal world is like slow motion; they can handle an overwhelming amount of work in seconds. Their physical flight speed is also incredibly high, almost like another species. However, their intelligence and wisdom remain the same—while they are fast at everything, without high spirituality, it's merely speed. The problem is that high-level spiritual cultivators complete almost all the work, leaving most others unemployed, simply waiting to be exploited.
But in the eyes of mortals, these cultivators are practically superhumans or heroes, almost on par with gods. However, the most terrifying thing is that they become so commonplace; after seeing them so often, people take these superhumans for granted.
In contrast to the high-ranking spiritual cultivators are the old nobles. While the old nobles may not be physically strong, they have held sway over time due to historical factors. However, in recent years, the old nobles have been gradually replaced. Their power has been increasingly challenged by the stronger individual high-ranking spiritual cultivators. The weak may briefly occupy those positions, but they generally cannot hold them. Strength is not a sufficient or necessary condition for power, but it is a necessary one. Therefore, high-ranking spiritual cultivators control most of the core thrones, and it is said that even the gods dare not forcibly offend them all. But is this truly the case?

The old nobles' claim that high-ranking spiritualists only weed out nobles who couldn't utilize them is a huge false premise. The weeding out process begins when the number of old nobles a spiritualist helps is far less than the number of positions it eliminates. Even those who can effectively utilize spiritualists will be eliminated, because the pyramids don't need so many skilled users; they only need a few exceptionally skilled ones. In the past, there were so many nobles because one person couldn't manage everything, but that's not the case today. Ultimately, only a small number of old nobles survive. Therefore, you'll find that an ordinary-looking mortal holds the lifeline of the divine kingdom.
