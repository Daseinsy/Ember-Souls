#  Luo Mingyuan's wife

Luo Mingyuan's wife is also a professor at the academy, but she teaches music. She comes from a prominent family in the Upper City District and is a well-known star in the Dream World.
However, she was beautiful, had a clear voice, and grew up in luxury, well-protected by her parents. She married Luo Mingyuan, a rising star from a commoner background, firmly binding him to her family's power. After marriage, she had a daughter, and her life could be described as perfect. She was a truly healing character, having never experienced hardship in her life.
She always spoke softly and gently, without any airs, and her classmates and colleagues all liked her; at least in most people's eyes, she was harmless. This also meant that she lacked empathy for certain kinds of suffering, and even if someone confided in her, they would be met with her calm expression and then swallow their words.
She had no concept of rules or injustice. In her eyes, the rules of this world had been the same since she was born, and she had never questioned them because she herself was a descendant well protected by the vested interests at the top of the pyramid.
