#  So-called spirituality

It is said that a person's happiness and well-being are controlled by their spirituality. If a person's spirituality is depleted, they will initially lose interest in many things and frantically search for things that bring them pleasure. Eventually, they will become completely unable to feel joy, and even lose all emotional fluctuations, becoming a person who can still move but is already dead. Legend has it that there are forbidden books that can directly control a person's spirituality, turning them into a talking tool.
It's not true that the more spiritual a person is, the happier they are. Legend has it that this is related to a certain aspect of spirituality, but scholars still haven't been able to figure out the secret.
