#  scattered

Just when I'm about to be slapped to death by a powerful being, he always flashes a sharp look in his eyes, then a wicked smile appears on his face, and the powerful being trips up by some unknown stone. If the powerful being is too troublesome, he'll suddenly have an emergency, or be manipulated by karma, etc. Oh, and he seems to have a spirit beast as a mount, but for some reason, this spirit beast seems very afraid of him, but it won't say when asked. When the protagonist is being criticized and manipulated, he suddenly smiles wickedly and then farts on the podium. When the protagonist wants to save someone... When the protagonist is filled with righteous indignation, he always accumulates hatred points before attacking. No one knows this secret of the protagonist, not even the protagonist himself. Those who knew are all dead. When the protagonist is looked down upon by a rich heiress, a hero always comes to the rescue, complete with a confidentiality agreement. The heiress, who used to look at him like he was a bug, assumes he's just acting. Those who know him well, seeing his blank, innocent expression, secretly curse, "This bastard's still putting on an act." The protagonist's calm demeanor means he never bats an eye when faced with difficulties, because he believes he's blessed by heaven and invincible. He always says, "I don't know, I really don't know." But because he's prone to losing limbs , he generally doesn't intentionally seek death, because that would truly hurt.
