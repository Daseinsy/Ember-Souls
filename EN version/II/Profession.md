A profession is the most exquisite and private thing in this world. Professions arise from need—a certain ability, skill, or service is required by living beings, and only then will the natural order recognize it as a profession. Professions not recognized by the natural order do not exist; even if you create your own name, you cannot obtain true professional power. History records thousands of professions: scholars, swordsmen, sword saints, kings, lords, instructors, hunters, inspectors, cleaners, philosophers, craftsmen, secretaries, rulers… and this does not include those unrecorded, and theoretically, new professions continue to emerge as time progresses.

A profession is essentially an art of living. Those who don't understand life may possess great psychic power, but their grasp of their profession is limited. A profession can manifest as text displayed above the head; no one knows the origin of this text, but every living being—including the blind—can understand its meaning. In combat, a profession automatically manifests, making it impossible to conceal an opponent's identity in a life-or-death struggle. However, a profession is extremely private information; in peacetime, no one will proactively display their profession, except in specific social settings—where respectable psychics display their profession as a symbol of status.

In theory, a person can cultivate multiple professions simultaneously, and each profession has its own independent experience and leveling system. Each profession has 100 levels, and upon reaching level 100, one can advance to a higher level, but no one knows what form they will take. For example, a Sword Saint is 99% of cultivators who are only one step away from advancing, yet they cannot predict what they will become after taking that step. This uncertainty makes professional cultivation both fascinating and risky; some are reborn during advancement, while others lose themselves in the process.

It's worth noting that having a profession inherently signifies nobility. Most commoners are unemployed. Those with professions, even if their psychic abilities aren't particularly strong, are treated as honored guests by various powerful factions. Even in wars of conquest, professionals gain a chance to survive due to their scarcity—they are talking tools, and tools are always more valuable than waste.

A profession is not something you are born with, but rather something formed by the recognition of the world. Legend has it that the recognition or observation of the world can cause disturbances in reality, thus giving rise to professional certification.
