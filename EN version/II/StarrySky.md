# StarrySky

In the Age of Doom, thousands of years ago, humanity had developed to an extremely high level of technology and had entered the era of interstellar colonization. The world's population had exploded, and conflicts between nations intensified. However, extinction weapons, far more dangerous than nuclear bombs, were abused without restraint, causing immense damage to the ecological balance of the universe. Hundreds of millions of lives were wiped out on a massive scale, and the morality and culture of society ultimately collapsed. At the end of the era, the universe began its spontaneous correction mechanism. Humanity was regarded as a virus by nature, natural laws changed, physical laws collapsed, and technological means became unusable. At the end of the era, humanity established the last gene pool, becoming the only remaining spark.
A starship stood tall in the unfathomable darkness of space, like a speck of white dust in a black ocean. The central control room was packed with people wearing high-precision headsets, rows of microcomputers before them. The electronic screens at the front displayed complex data and the operational status of the spacecraft. Excitement and anticipation, tinged with a hint of uncertainty, were written on their faces. The final moment had arrived. The numbers on the screen steadily decreased, like the last fireworks of civilization. Here, there were no more classes or distinctions; everyone sat together, awaiting the final moment. Beep, beep, beep—the slow, deep electronic clock announced the final hour. Three, two, one—seeds assembled. With the command given, a small capsule launched from the ship, disappearing rapidly into the boundless space with a faint glow. People smiled with relief. No one spoke. Some slowly closed their eyes, others stared blankly at the screen, quietly, quietly awaiting that eternal peace.
Centuries ago, the supernatural forces that the ancients believed to be supernatural were first collectively discovered by humankind. People discovered that the flow of human thought contained unimaginable power, which could even communicate with the laws of the world and change the genetic sequence of one's own body in order to achieve the goal of creating superhuman beings. Breathing exercises, spiritual cultivation, and meditation were proposed as cultivation methods.
Fire was humanity's ancient hope for survival; today, lamplight has replaced the flame for illumination, and no one can embrace that ancient hope anymore.
Today marks the second year since humans moved here. The sky here is always in extremes, with hazy clouds and clear light intertwined. The seasons are so long that it seems as if one has lost the concept of time, wanting to forget the entire century. Each era has its own color, and this also points to the souls living on this land.
The vast starry sky, the boundless earth, and in the distance, tiny worm-like creatures crawl on the ground, perhaps better described as crawling. Although they are walking, what is the point?
Skyscrapers have to rise from the ground to meet the desire to soar to the sky. People like quick and brutal cultivation, but forget that the gentle flow of time is the eternal rhythm of nature. Little by little, the long river of time flows through here, reflecting the fragmented images of generations.
Some people live in trees until they themselves become tree people, connected to the earth. The earth is indeed vast, but it does not want to lose the ocean and the sky. Can trees roam freely in the ocean? Or will they take root in the starry sky, embracing the soul within the endless universe?
Legend has it that the souls of the dead ascend to the sky, but no one knows what is there. Even though the buildings of the divine kingdom stretch to the highest point in the sky, the sky seems to be endless.
