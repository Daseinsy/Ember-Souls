#  mad Men

The world was deathly silent, bathed in a uniform white light—the silent eye of heaven, hanging high in the sky, gazing down upon the earth. A tiny figure, as insignificant as a grain of sand, struggled forward amidst the dizzying light and wind. He had no idea how long he had been moving forward; his mind was a jumble of thoughts, like a tangled mess of yarn, impossible to unravel.
He couldn't remember why he was moving forward, but there was a vague voice in his mind, as if it were bursting out from the depths of his soul: Search, keep searching, even if you die a thousand deaths, even if you dissipate into dust carried away by the wind.
He raised his hands, the flame of hope flickering in his chest, his thoughts burning, his life ignited, and he could already see the eternal silence waving to him.
His brush moved with the speed and fluidity of a dragon, the blade danced in his palm. He longed to record something, to solidify the intangible things in his mind into visible phenomena. Closer still, his eyes burned with fervent passion, and everything around him faded away madly before his eyes, leaving only the painting that was about to be completed.
The madman was once a well-behaved and sensible child, but an invasion of evil spirits caused his spiritual awakening prematurely. He then began to care about the suffering of the world and to ask why. In his youth, he had the good fortune to enter a space and converse with the spirit of a sage.
Then he gave up his job at the college, became a freak in his parents' eyes, and embarked on a lonely path of asceticism.
Because the madman realized from the bottom of his heart that if even his own parents and relatives could not understand him, let alone anyone else, he regarded relationships as something toxic and harmful, and he disdained to associate with them. Although he suffered all kinds of abuse and contempt, he never really bent his back and became the kind of person he hated. He never would have done so before, and he never will.
Years later, his former relatives had long since died for various reasons or vanished without a trace, while the madman was the one who survived to the end and became the strongest. Time proved that his choice back then was not wrong. If he had stayed there, he would either have become the kind of person he hated or fought to the end, perishing along with that corrupt environment.

He had no home, but made the world his home. He was a chivalrous knight-errant, even living among wild beasts. Sometimes he would stay in one place for a long time, but he would eventually leave. He felt that he could never find his place in this world, or rather, he knew that he should not belong to any particular identity or place. He always satisfied the thirst in his heart through endless wandering. He was incredibly powerful, and he didn't know whether he had lived for hundreds or thousands of years, because he didn't care about time at all. He was strong enough to walk freely in the wilderness. He didn't sleep because he suffered from insomnia. His soul could never find peace. Perhaps this was fate's punishment for his rebellion. He had witnessed the rise and fall of city-states, the changing of history, helped countless people, and in doing so, received even a little bit of quenching in return.
His profession has changed countless times; some people think he was a butcher or something, but as people forget about him, his profession keeps changing.
"You are mad," several titles appeared above his head: Destroyer, Demon Symbiote, Scarlett's Butcher. Manifestation, Primordial, Ascension, Disintegration, Chaos, Twisting, Annihilation, Creation. Last night, the west wind withered the green, alone I climbed the high tower, gazing to the ends of the earth. My clothes grow looser, yet I have no regrets, for I am wasting away for her. Suddenly I turn around, and there she is, in the dim light. Every step forward in cultivation is a step closer to madness. Entropy shrouds all things. The price of power is madness and destruction.

A small figure shivered in the cold wind. Only a girl, her hand held by her mother's, ran over and gave him some food. He remembered that girl, a memory that would last a lifetime. "Mommy, why is that brother alone on the street? Is he lost?" The girl's mother looked at her daughter's innocent face and sighed inwardly. Abandoned orphans were everywhere, struggling to survive for their own families. Who would care about the life or death of a homeless child on the street? Ru Kuang, driven mad with hunger, tried to steal food, but the stall owner discovered him and beat him severely. Covered in bruises, he huddled on the street, his vision blurring. He seemed to see his dead mother. No, it was a little girl who gave him the bread from her hand.
He was taken in by an old woman in a black robe who saw the spiritual light in him and took him as her disciple. This old woman had betrayed the Longevity Sect and was captured and killed by him with his own hands. Because of them, they had made a promise long ago that they must live well. At that moment, he lowered his head and remained silent, with only the sound of the wind around him and the old woman's closed-eye body.
The man in black patted Ru Kuang on the shoulder and laughed loudly, "This is truly an outstanding prospect for my Changsheng Sect."

This is a follower of demons, and because of his worship of demons, he has gained a certain degree of demonic protection. He can move freely in the wilderness, and most evil spirits will not attack demons because they lack hatred towards them.

This organization aims to bring a new order to the world, believing that demons should be the true rulers of the world, and to establish a world where demons and evil spirits coexist.

The Cult of Longevity is a place where all its followers are obsessed with immortality. They plunder the world's resources at all costs, drawing pleasure from them to combat loneliness and madness. These are madmen who have taken the preservation of their own integrity to the extreme. Wherever they appear, mass death occurs. They wear black robes and masks, and it is said that even members of the cult do not know each other's identities. Legend has it that they are always accompanied by the Death-Summoning Bird.

Legend has it that evil spirits originate from humans harboring immense resentment. They linger in the places where they lived, and driven by their intense resentment, they hunt all living things with ruthless abandon. The presence of legendary evil spirits creates a malevolent realm, governed by inexplicable rules; only by abiding by these rules can one survive. Resentment and suffering can make evil spirits even more powerful.
Demons are a species from the Chaos Realm. Because their world is extremely barren, they are eager to conquer other worlds. They are cruel killing machines. They can draw life from living beings. Legend has it that the reason they are so keen on invasion is that they cannot reproduce or feed. Their only living army consists of the new demons that are periodically conceived in the Chaos Realm. At the center of their world is a Chaos Tree. Offering sacrifices to it, all demons are born from it. Their only nourishment is living creatures.

The church believes in God, a real deity, a female deity, whose image is bowed and bound to a cross. Legend says the cross is a sacred object used to seal demons; God used her own body to seal the demons, saving humanity. Therefore, the cross is a symbol of redemption and hope, while the inverted cross is a symbol of evil. However, God is actually a prisoner. She wanted to help humanity, and thus humanity could use holy water and holy light to dispel evil. But God is not perfect; a part of her body houses a demon. Having long listened to prayers for redemption and witnessed various evil thoughts in humanity, this demon formed an evil thought—a manifestation of God herself—wanting to rule the world and enslave all living beings. The dragon slayer becomes the dragon himself; it was Jesus who, at the cost of his own life, awakened God. Jesus moved God, awakening her goodness. To seal this evil thought, God used the cross to seal herself away. Her good side consistently helps humanity, but sometimes her evil side prevails, which is why the cross is inverted. Because of God's duality, the church is also dualistic. On the one hand, it punishes evil and helps mankind; on the other hand, it is a place where officials and scoundrels operate, even though the church tends to favor the lawful side.

Knowledge cannot save humanity, nor can spiritual power. In fact, while spiritual power grants humanity immense strength, this strength comes at the cost of madness. In the hands of good people, spiritual power can be used for good; in the hands of evil, it is used to satisfy selfish desires. The ultimate evil in the world is entropy, which is the opposite of creation. As a tendency towards destruction, it is an objective law of the world. If living beings are the result of creation, then evil thoughts, evil spirits, and demons are the result of entropy. Entropy is the shadow of creation; wherever there is creation, there is entropy, just as wherever there are living beings, there are conflicts, disputes, and demons. However, living beings exist precisely to combat entropy; they always persist in hope amidst hopeless nothingness, and our world is born from this.
