#  Divine Kingdom Research Institute

The Divine Kingdom Research Institute is always conducting various investigations and trying various methods. Its headquarters are located within the academy, but it is also a powerful institution, because absolute research is always accompanied by absolute power.

For example, it has diplomatic immunity. It has the right to make social contacts. It has the right to collect information. It has the right to summon investigators.

When the Divine Kingdom Research Institute arrests people under the pretext of official business, ordinary people almost cannot refuse.
