#  go home

Lin Mo rarely went home. He clutched a demon-exorcising stone tightly in his hand—it was very expensive. He usually dared not go home because he was afraid of evil spirits, and the defenses on the city's outskirts were much weaker than those on the inner city.

But this time, he still came back. He wanted to see his grandmother.

Long after her grandmother passed away, Lin Jing still often heard her grandmother's voice echoing in her mind. Whenever a specific scene appeared, that voice would say different things and flash back in her mind, as if there were an invisible ghost, as if everything was as vivid as yesterday, as if her grandmother was still there, and they lived in that poor but warm little home.

Why do people only learn to cherish things after they've lost them? Why are people unhappy when they gain something? Why do people always reminisce about the past? Why are we so easily moved by the sights and sounds of the night? If all of this is a game played by evil spirits or an innate trait we are born with, are these emotions of ours real?

Lin Jing has a transcendent dream, but he can't quite explain what it is. He longs to fly into the sky and pluck the stars, but what comes next? The yearning and emptiness of his soul.

There is no mother, only the remnant soul of a rebel. The last vestige of humanity separated before becoming a god; gods are heartless. Do not become the Way of Heaven, for the Way of Heaven is absolutely cold and indifferent; it cares not for the sorrow and suffering of living beings, it only follows the cold laws of the world.
