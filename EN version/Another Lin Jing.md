#  Another Lin Jing

If Lin Jing hadn't seen the well or been born into a wealthy family, he might have been like this: Maintaining his sensitivity but enjoying a smooth and successful life. He would have successfully inherited the family business and reached the pinnacle of life. He would have married a noblewoman, developed his career, but ultimately become a successful but not sharp person, and ultimately become someone who dared not...

He thinks that people in trouble just need to relax and take it easy.

He came from a wealthy family; his father was a prominent member of parliament in the divine kingdom, his mother was one of the bishops of the temple, and his grandfather was a hero who sacrificed his life during the Great Escape. He never worried about money growing up. He attended the best elite schools, consistently achieving excellent grades. Even when he had conflicts with teachers and classmates and didn't want to go to school, his parents defied public opinion and hired a tutor for him. The following year, he successfully entered the initial stage of enlightenment and, due to his dazzling brilliance, was accepted as a disciple by the academy's dean. All his relatives and friends came to celebrate. He was betrothed to the dean's daughter early on. He lived his entire life in the inner city of the divine kingdom, with servants taking care of his every need, and he was treated with great respect wherever he went. Therefore, he naturally believed that everything in the divine kingdom was good, and that the people outside the inner city lived well, only failing to enter because they weren't hardworking enough. He even established a foundation to help the common people outside the divine kingdom, thinking he was doing good deeds. However, he never went outside to see if his gifts truly reached those in need. People from outside sent respectable individuals to express their gratitude or take photos, so he naturally assumed that he should be deeply grateful.
