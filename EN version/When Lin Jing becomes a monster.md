#  When Lin Jing becomes a monster

When Lin Jing discovers that his life was a script meticulously crafted, when he discovers he comes from the abyss, a tentacle used by an evil god to destroy the world, when he discovers that everyone who cared for him and loved him was merely a tool to be manipulated, when he discovers that the evil spirits themselves have exposed the ugliness of the world to him, causing him to lose trust in humanity and leading him to destroy everything and bring civilization to an end, when he discovers that everything he believed in has finally collapsed, what will he do? He does nothing. He simply closes his eyes, thinks for a moment, and then calmly opens them. His eyes still hold confusion, pain, shyness, and unease, but he is no longer bound by them.

Ah Chen, who could also see the well, chose madness and destruction, because he wanted to destroy the humanity he distrusted, to destroy this hopeless world that insulted them and would not treat them kindly. The evil spirits' goal was not to save the world, but to exterminate all of humanity and establish a new world.

Even after becoming a monster, Ah Chen still hesitates to harm children and adults who are like children.
