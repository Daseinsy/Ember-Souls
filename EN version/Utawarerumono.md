#  Utawarerumono

The truly memorable things are never in parliaments, assemblies, sealed libraries, or portraits for people to admire, but live in people's hearts. Even if they have no name or form, they are engraved in the souls of the right people.

Lin Jing's grandmother has passed away, and perhaps no one but Lin Jing remembers her anymore. But he knows that the spirit is part of humanity, and as long as Lin Jing continues to shine, that legacy will be passed down.
