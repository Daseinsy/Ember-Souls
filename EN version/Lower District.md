#  Lower District

The story uses the characters' perspectives to depict the lives of people in the lower city.
The children studied diligently day and night, spending all their time in the library.
The adults are listless, spending their days idly. Aside from socializing and cultivating relationships, they merely absorb spiritual energy. Those slightly better off have professions, but these are limited to food, drink, and transportation, as they are cheaper than the spiritual cultivators in these areas. News of people being corrupted by evil spirits circulates from time to time, instilling a deep-seated insecurity in the people. They huddle together for warmth at night, unsure if they will see the sun rise the next day. The poor have many children; if a dragon or phoenix is born, it's the expected outcome. Either the whole family eats well and enjoys safe protection.
Although it's really hard to starve to death and one can maintain basic survival even without doing anything, most people here are unwilling to be mediocre, unwilling to be looked down upon by their relatives, unwilling to be randomly killed by evil spirits one day, and unwilling to not have more interesting dreams or a more powerful realm.
