#  Relics

Spiritual relics are super-condensations of spirit. They may arise from historical events, legends, myths, epics, or family inheritances. Regardless of the source, they are the epitome of spirit, containing power beyond human limits. The most powerful spiritual relics are known as divine artifacts, mostly controlled by powerful families. "Below divine artifacts, all are ants"—this is a universally acknowledged ironclad rule in the cultivation world. Even if the possessor of a divine artifact is only at the initial stage of spiritual energy, they are capable of contending with cultivators at the transcendent or even radiant levels. The power of a spiritual relic does not depend on the user's spiritual energy strength, but rather on the compatibility between the user and the relic.

Spiritual relics are said to be indestructible—ordinary attacks are unlikely to destroy them, but in reality, they can still be buried if subjected to sufficiently powerful attacks or the aftershocks of battle. Historically, many powerful spiritual relics have been shattered or permanently depleted in battles at the Sovereign Realm. Spiritual relics possess the characteristic of "self-concealment": without a master, they remain in a state of obscurity, appearing indistinguishable from ordinary objects, making them extremely difficult to discover, and requiring specific methods to activate. Once, a sacred-level spiritual relic stood on a bustling street in a grand city, touched, examined, and even kicked by countless people, yet its true identity remained undiscovered for millennia. It wasn't until a wandering child happened to touch the correct spot on a rainy night that this dormant heritage was awakened.

Some spiritual relics exist in the starry sky, never falling to earth. They float in the void, like silent stars. However, these spiritual relics may automatically choose their masters—directly recognizing a master at a suitable juncture of destiny. These extremely rare individuals are known as the Chosen Ones, those blessed by the heavens. The birth of a Chosen One is unpredictable; they might be an orphan from the slums or an heir to a family. Destiny doesn't consider birth, but only whether the wavelength of their spirituality resonates with that spiritual relic.

Every spirit relic has its own story—its very soul. The contractor is not a "master," but a "resonator." The process of signing a contract is called "resonance." Discovering the story behind a spirit relic means accumulating affinity: the higher the affinity, the greater the power it can unleash. When the affinity is high enough, the contractor can master a power called "Liberation Skill"—a skill with devastating effects, said to be able to mobilize the ultimate spiritual energy of heaven and earth to generate a powerful impact. However, after this, the spirit relic will be exhausted and fall into a state of prolonged inactivity, ranging from several months to decades.

Artifacts are categorized into Sacred Artifacts and Evil Artifacts based on their characteristics. Sacred Artifacts are typically the embodiment of positive intentions such as justice, protection, and creation; Evil Artifacts, on the other hand, originate from negative intentions such as killing, deception, and destruction. Neither is inherently good or evil—Sacred Artifacts, if in the hands of evil individuals, can also be used for murder, and Evil Artifacts, if wielded by benevolent individuals, may protect those in need. However, Evil Artifacts subtly influence the user's character during use, and long-term holders often experience significant personality changes.

Legend has it that some divine artifacts were weapons of the gods, perishing along with their masters during Ragnarok, their extinguished spirits disappearing into the starry sky. If a being can find and unleash the true power of these artifacts, they may gain new authority—becoming a new god. This is a shortcut to godhood, but also the most dangerous. For these artifacts are called divine because they carry the will and obsessions of their original owners . To attempt to wield a divine artifact is to wrestle with a dead god. Win, and you gain divine power; lose, and you are devoured by the god, becoming part of the artifact.
