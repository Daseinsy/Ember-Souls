#  The Fallen Aristocracy

She was deceived. Her family fortune was embezzled by the steward, her parents were trampled by a giant beast in a conflict, and she fled into the wilderness, pursued by the fangs of a shadow. The beast swallowed her whole, and as she lay dying in the darkness, she felt something tearing through her fabric on her back—wings, radiant with light, bursting forth from the flesh of her shoulder blades. She transformed into light, bursting through the beast's belly, leaving a burning trail in the sky like a newborn star.
