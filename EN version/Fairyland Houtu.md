#  Fairyland Houtu

Legend has it that there exists a verdant forest, its air fresh and teeming with life, a veritable hidden paradise on earth. No one knows its location, only that some have stumbled upon it on foot. In reality, this paradise's tranquility comes at the cost of isolation; it is self-sufficient and lives in peace, seemingly perfect. However, it is inherently unsustainable. External invasions cannot prevent isolation, and internal conflicts will ultimately lead to the invasion. As long as desires exist in the human heart, a truly pure Eden cannot exist.

This is a land nearly destroyed by trauma, inhabited by creatures orphaned by Ragnarok, who no longer trust humans or the outside world. Perhaps due to their peaceful nature, the elves and spirit beasts have coexisted peacefully for countless years, making this place seem like paradise. However, these immortal beings witnessed their kin being torn apart in war, leaving indelible scars, and thus isolated themselves from the world. They also refuse to take in humans, believing that humans are born burdened with original sin, a sin they, being sensitive beings, can always see in them. When technology encounters humans, the best course of action is to allow them to live permanently until their natural lifespan ends, depriving them of the right to reproduce, as they distrust the offspring of these humans. If the elves encounter a human and see the mission they bear, they will blindfold and deprive them of their senses, send them away, and then sign a secret pact, even relocating them for added security.

The reincarnation plot also exists in this world, but it is not reincarnation in another world. Instead, it is reincarnation across oceans and across time and space. A person who was originally in another city-state may be thrown into a world far away on the other side of the ocean, where even the flow of time is different.
