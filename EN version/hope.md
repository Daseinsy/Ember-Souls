#  hope

This world is so hopeless, so what kind of hope do I want to depict? It's the love of an old person for a child, the resilience and courage to struggle for survival in disaster, the clean and resolute face of a young girl, the sage who understands the great path and forges light, the firewood that, even when stabbed, still cares for humanity, the children who can see the well, and the faceless ones. It's the thunderous strike of "Let my inner universe erupt!" against the world. It's the phrase "I want to live." It's the courage to plan and wield the sword in the face of injustice. It's the quiet and peaceful village when nothing is happening.
