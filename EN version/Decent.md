# Decent

Recently, a trend has emerged among the nobility: they travel to various scenic spots, even paying exorbitant sums to ensure their safety before venturing into the wilderness to enjoy the scenery. Then, their attendants bring professional photographers to record everything, which is then posted or donated to the exhibition halls of the divine kingdom, allowing the world to admire their splendor. Even more impressively, they place these recordings in the library as materials for cultivation, although these materials are embellished with some personal touches. But since they are precious wilderness recordings, ordinary people really can't refuse them.

Among the nobility, it was even fashionable to determine their social standing by how many of their private paintings were selected, thus giving rise to a new profession: the portrait painter.
