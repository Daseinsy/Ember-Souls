#  Instance: Wilderness Internship

Entering the wilderness, characters, leading a team, danger, treasure, things.

Most of the mentors leading the teams don't really care about the lives of the students, but the academy does. No, the academy doesn't care either, but the students are precious talents whose lives will be directly questioned by the gods and the public. Therefore, it must ensure that the mortality rate is not too high each time. It doesn't matter if one or two die occasionally, but the deaths must be reasonable and able to withstand investigation, and cannot be attributed to the mentor's dereliction of duty.
The team is led by two guides: a master and a temple overseer, along with a senior deacon, and finally the trainees who need to be trained.
